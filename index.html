Supervised Classification (Hover/Tap image for description)

This map visualizes a pixel-based land cover classification, effectively categorizing the region into four distinct surface types: Green (Vegetation), Paved (Impervious Surfaces), Sparse (Mixed/Transition), and Water. The resulting map highlights the spatial distribution of urban infrastructure and ecological zones.

Data Source:
The imagery for this analysis was acquired from the European Space Agency’s (ESA) Sentinel-2 satellite program. Sentinel-2 optical remote sensing data is highly effective for land mapping due to its 13 spectral bands and high spatial resolution of up to 10 meters.

Methodology: Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence
The categorization of this landscape was achieved through supervised classification using machine learning techniques.

Heavy Metal (Lead) Spatial Distribution Study — Arroceros Forest Park (Thesis Adviser)

  • Conducted field sampling of soil and foliar dust to quantify lead contamination across spatial points.
  • Coordinated laboratory extraction and trace-metal analysis using atomic absorption spectroscopy (AAS).
  • Modeled environmental drivers of heavy metal accumulation including plant height, species type, and distance from pollution sources.
  • Integrated statistical outputs into spatial interpolation models to identify contamination hotspots.
  • Generated findings supporting environmental monitoring and mitigation planning.
