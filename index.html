Key Projects Handled & Research Conducted

• International Urban Systems Project (2025): Designed spatial databases combining global datasets (GHSL, WorldPop, SCAD/ACLED conflict data), developed hierarchical zone classification models using distance and urban morphology indicators, and implemented automated Python geospatial pipelines.

• SAFER U Project - Project NOAH (2022): Processed and validated geospatial climate datasets at the UP Resilience Institute to support climate change adaptation and disaster risk reduction planning for state universities.

• Heavy Metal (Lead) Spatial Distribution Study (2024): Directed field sampling of soil and foliar dust at Arroceros Forest Park, coordinated AAS laboratory extraction, and modeled environmental drivers and interpolation hotspots.

• Metro Manila Landscape Connectivity Thesis (2021): Modeled urban ecological corridors and habitat fragmentation indices using the Grainscape and raster packages in R for University of Debrecen.
